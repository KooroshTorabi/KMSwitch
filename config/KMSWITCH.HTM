Screens

Arrange the two PCs like monitors in your OS display settings: drag PC 2 to where its screens are on your desk. It snaps to PC 1's edges. The pointer crosses only where the screens touch (blue); everywhere else the edge stops it, exactly like between two monitors of one PC. Each PC is drawn with its real monitors as measured.

Switching

How the box moves between the two PCs.

Hotkeys

Pick modifiers and a key, or "none" to turn a hotkey off. The PC never sees these keys. WIN is the Windows key (Command on a Mac).

Firmware

Updates install on both microcontrollers from this PC. Your settings stay as they are. Details: docs/firmware-update.md.
